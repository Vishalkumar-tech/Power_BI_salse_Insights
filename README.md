# Sales Spotlight 📊

## Overview
This project presents a detailed analysis of sales data across various markets and timelines. It focuses on identifying key business drivers such as the **top-performing customer**, **most sold product**, and **sales performance in 2020**. The goal is to enable data-driven decision-making through clear visual storytelling and trend breakdowns.

## Key Insights
- 🥇 **Top Customer:** `Prod239` with over 240K units purchased.
- 🛒 **Highest Selling Product:** `Electricalsara Stores` with 650K+ units sold.
- 📅 **Focus Year:** 2020 – highlighting post-pandemic sales behavior and regional trends.

## Project Highlights
- 💰 **Total Revenue Analyzed:** ₹984.81 Million  
- 📦 **Total Sales Quantity:** 2 Million+ units  
- 📍 **Top Markets:** Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Ahmedabad  
- 🏆 **Top Products:** Electricalsara Stores, Premium Stores, Excel Stores  
- 👥 **Top Customers:** Prod239, Prod090, Prod237

## Visualized Dashboards Include:
- Revenue by Market (bar graph)
- Sales Quantity by Product
- Top Products and Customers
- Revenue Trend (2017–2020)
- Detailed sales data from Jan 2018 to Jan 2020

## Tools & Technologies Used
